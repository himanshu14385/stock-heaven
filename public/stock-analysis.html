Buy Zone — Column Guide

Simple language mein har column kya check karta hai.

StockCompany ka naam aur NSE symbol.
PriceAbhi ka current market price aur aaj ka change.
SMA 20Last 20 trading sessions ka average price. Price iske upar ho to short-term trend stronger maana jata hai.
SMA 50Last 50 sessions ka average. Medium-term trend ka simple indicator.
SMA 200Last 200 sessions ka average. Long-term trend ka broad picture deta hai.
RSIMomentum 0–100 scale par. 30 ke neeche oversold, 70 ke upar overbought. Oversold alone buy confirmation nahi hai.
VWAPVolume ko include karke average traded price. Price VWAP ke upar ho to buyers relatively stronger.
MACDMomentum aur trend change check karta hai. Positive histogram bullish momentum, negative bearish momentum.
BollingerPrice ki recent volatility/range. Lower band ke paas oversold pressure, upper band ke paas overbought pressure; middle band trend bias mein help karta hai.
VolumeAaj ka volume recent average se compare hota hai. 1.5×+ strong participation, 0.8× se neeche weak participation.
Confirmation8 bullish checks: Price vs SMA20/50/200, healthy RSI, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger middle aur volume. Example 6/8 = 8 mein se 6 checks supportive.
Technical Score100-point technical score. SMA20/50/200 = 15-15-15, RSI = 10, MACD = 15, VWAP = 10, Bollinger = 10, Volume = 10. Missing data ko unfairly zero nahi kiya jata; available weights re-normalise hote hain.
Support / Buy RangeRecent swing lows aur ATR volatility se nearest meaningful support estimate hota hai. Buy range support ke around practical entry area hai; guarantee nahi.
Zone coloursStrong Buy = price fair value se ≤80%; Buy = 80–95%; Hold = 95–110%; Sell = >110%.
Important: Valuation Zone aur Technical Confirmation alag cheezein hain. Cheap stock ko technical confirmation na mile to blindly buy signal nahi maana jana chahiye.
